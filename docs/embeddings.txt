An embedding is a list of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text. Texts with similar meaning produce vectors that point in similar directions.

Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors rather than the distance between their endpoints. It is a common way to compare embeddings because it ignores how long the vectors are.

Chunking splits a long document into smaller passages before embedding, because a single vector cannot represent a long document well. Fixed-size chunking is predictable, while paragraph chunking preserves complete ideas in well-structured text.
